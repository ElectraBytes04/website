[Notes] Partitioning Digital Storage

2026-05-11

TL;DR:

  • Convert your device's capacity to binary units
  • Align partitions to MiB (first partition starting at 1MiB) (that's MeBibytes, not Megabytes)
  • Make sure a partition has enough space for necessary filesystem headers (LUKS2 headers require ~16MiB).
