======================
Banana Oatmeal Cookies
======================
.. class:: sub

2025-12-21

------

Yield: ~36 Cookies

Ingredients
```````````
- 3 Bananas
- 1/2 Cup Brown Sugar
- 1/4 Cup Sugar
- 3/4 Cup (Room Temperature) Butter
- 1 Large Egg
- 1 Tablespoon Vanilla
- (A Small Amount of) Honey
- 2 Cup Unbleached All Purpose Flour
- 1/2 Teaspoon Baking Soda
- 1 Teaspoon Salt
- 1 Teaspoon Cinnamon
- 2 1/2 Cup Quick Oats
- 3/4 Cups Chocolate Chips


Notes
`````
| I used raw honey since that's all I had.
| Rolled oats should also work, instead of quick oats.


Baking
``````
| In your favorite stand mixer:
|     Cream the butter, sugar, and brown sugar together.
|     Mix the bananas, vanilla, honey, and egg in.
| 
| In a separate bowl:
|     Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, oatmeal, cinnamon.
| 
| Pour the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients and mix.
| 
| Add the chocolate chips.
| 
| Scoop and press each ball down slightly.
| Bake at 375°F for 10 minutes.
